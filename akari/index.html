Surround to purify

Your path is your power. There is no sword and no shooting: move around whatever you want to heal and close the circle with your magic trail. Everything caught inside is purified. A still tree is easy — an enemy that chases you, clubs you or spits venom is not.

  • Free the creatures

    The Oni swing their clubs and the Hebi, water snakes, spit venom. Purify them so they recover their true nature and can leave in peace.

  • Beware of the Kegare

    Impurity itself, walking. It corrupts again the trees and Oni you had already healed — act before it undoes your work.

  • Don't trust what you see

    The Kitsune create illusions to confuse you. Some trees and Oni are not what they seem: purify them and they vanish.

  • Beat your time

    No score, just the clock. Sharpen your movement, find more efficient routes and finish each level in the shortest time possible.
